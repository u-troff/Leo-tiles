## WHO WE ARE

R&R Plumbing Services is a family-owned, licensed & insured plumbing business serving the Los Angeles, CA area. Rafael, the founder, brings more than two decades of plumbing experience. His extensive plumbing experience has exposed him to it all, from small residential repairs to multimillion-dollar commercial projects. Under R&R, Rafael works with an elite team of well-prepared technicians, who follow his business philosophy of providing honest, quality work. As he likes to say, quoting Henry Ford, “Quality means doing it right when no one is looking.”
