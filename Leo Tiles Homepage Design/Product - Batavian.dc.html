The range

Our signature hand-shaped tile — mixed, pressed and edged by hand, then fired to a natural terracotta red with the tonal variation you'd expect from real clay. Suited to floors, decks and pool surrounds, indoors and out.

Sizes 300×300mm standard · custom sizes on request
Thickness ≈ 25mm
Recommended joint 25mm outdoors · 10–15mm indoors (pre-sorted)
Grout Sand-cement or flexible grout for wide joints
Coverage ≈ 9 tiles per m² at 300×300mm, 25mm joint
Natural tone range

Tone varies with each tile's position in the kiln — expect a natural mix within any order, not a flat, uniform colour.
