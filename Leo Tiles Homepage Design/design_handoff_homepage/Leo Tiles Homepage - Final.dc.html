Our story

A family-owned kiln, rooted in craft

Leo Tiles began in Kraaifontein with little more than a kiln, a passion for clay, and Mark Kelly's determination to do things the hard, honest way. What started as a small workshop has grown into South Africa's most trusted source for handmade terracotta — but the process has never changed.

Mark has spent over 50 years perfecting the art of handmade terracotta tile manufacturing — bespoke, kiln-fired terracotta used in homes, heritage restorations and architectural projects across the country and abroad.

Today, Leo Tiles supplies homeowners, architects and restoration projects across Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria, Durban, Stellenbosch and beyond — every tile still shaped and fired by hand in the same Cape Town kiln.

  • Handmade from raw clay since the 1970s
  • Kiln-fired in our own Cape Town facility
  • Custom sizes manufactured to specification
  • Supply and installation available nationwide
Common Problems

Tile problems solved with care and craft

Factory tiles lack character

Mass-produced tiles look identical. Handmade terracotta develops warmth, variation and patina that no factory can replicate.

Long lead times catch people off guard

Handmade tiles take time. We're upfront about production schedules — contact us early in your project.

Wrong installer ruins the result

Non-standard sizing requires specialist installation. Our own team has installed thousands of square metres of our tiles.

Unsealed tiles stain permanently

Terracotta must be sealed correctly before and after grouting. We supply the right sealant and advise on process.

Custom size quotes take weeks

We respond to custom enquiries fast. Tell us your project dimensions and we'll confirm feasibility and pricing quickly.

Grout that clashes visually

White grout kills the warmth of terracotta. We supply colour-matched grout in natural tones.
