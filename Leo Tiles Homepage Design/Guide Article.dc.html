For installers

Choosing the right joint width for outdoor terracotta

A technical guide for installers and site teams working with our standard 300×300mm terracotta outdoors. Five steps, from understanding size variation to the final dry-lay check.

01

Understand the size variation

Because every tile is hand pressed and shaped, expect a natural size variation of approximately 6–8% across a batch. This isn't a defect to sort out — it's the reason the joint needs to be wide enough to absorb it.

02

Use a 25mm joint outdoors

For our standard 300×300mm tile laid outdoors, we recommend a 25mm joint as the average. At this spacing, coverage works out to roughly 9 tiles per m² — which is also why we sell by the tile rather than by the box.

03

Narrower joints, indoors only

A tighter 10–15mm joint can work indoors, but only if tiles are pre-sorted for size at the factory before dispatch. Ask us to grade a batch tighter if you're planning a narrow joint.

04

Match your grout to the joint

Wider joints call for a sand-cement or flexible grout rather than a rigid, fine-line product — rigid grout is more likely to crack under the natural movement of a wide outdoor joint.

05

Dry-lay before you fix

Always lay out a small section dry first. It lets you see the real tone and size variation in that batch and adjust spacing before anything is set permanently.
