Case Study 01

Luxury Terrace

Situated overlooking the valley, this project utilized our hand-pressed terracotta to create a seamless transition between the indoor living space and the infinity pool surround. The natural variations in the clay mirror the rugged mountain landscape.

LOCATION: STELLENBOSCH, ZA

SPEC: CLASSIC RECTANGLE, 150X300MM

Case Study 02

Residential Courtyard

A modern minimalist courtyard designed as a sanctuary of light. Our herringbone pattern creates a rhythmic ground plane that anchors the architectural volume of the main residence while maintaining a warm, organic texture underfoot.

LOCATION: CAPE TOWN, ZA

SPEC: SLIM BRICK, 50X200MM (HERRINGBONE)

Case Study 03

Commercial Wine Estate

Designed for high traffic without sacrificing artisanal soul. This estate chose our extra-fired pavers for their durability and "kiln-glow" patina, which deepens and matures beautifully with the estate's world-class vintage.

LOCATION: FRANSCHHOEK, ZA

SPEC: HEAVY DUTY SQUARE, 400X400MM

Heritage Restorations

"Leo Tiles provided the only match for our 18th-century masonry restoration. The color depth and structural integrity were indistinguishable from the original site remains."

Restoring historical landmarks requires a deep understanding of 1975 firing techniques. We work with conservationists to reproduce heritage profiles that honor the original architectural intent.
