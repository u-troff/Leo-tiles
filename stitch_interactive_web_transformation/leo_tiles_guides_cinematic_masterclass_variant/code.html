The Foundation
Substrate

Ensure the substrate is fully cured, structurally sound, and free of contaminants. For terracotta, a reinforced cement screed with a moisture content of < 3% is mandatory. Leveling must be within 3mm over a 3m span to prevent 'lippage' in large format layouts.

The Bond
Adhesion

Apply a high-flexibility (S1 or S2 rated) cementitious adhesive. Due to the natural porosity of terracotta, we mandate the 'back-buttering' technique to ensure 100% coverage, preventing hollow spots that can lead to failure under point-loads.
