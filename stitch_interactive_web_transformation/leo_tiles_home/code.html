THE PROCESS

From Kiln to Courtyard.

Unlike factory-made tiles, no two Leo Tiles are identical. The subtle variation in tone and texture is what gives them character and warmth.

01

Manual Shaping

Raw clay is wedged and pressed into wooden molds by hand, ensuring the structural integrity of the ceramic body.

02

Slow Drying

Tiles are air-dried in our Cape Town facility, allowing moisture to escape naturally to prevent cracking during firing.

03

Traditional Firing

Kiln temperatures are meticulously monitored to achieve the signature patina and iron-rich terracotta hues.
