Professional Standards

Installation Guide

01. Substrate Prep

Ensure the surface is structurally sound, level, and cured. Terracotta requires a moisture-free base to prevent efflorescence after installation.

02. Adhesive Choice

Use a high-performance, rapid-set flexible adhesive. Our tiles are highly porous; the correct bonding agent is critical for thermal expansion.

03. Joint Spacing

We recommend a 6mm to 10mm joint to celebrate the natural variation in tile dimensions. This traditional aesthetic also allows for building movement.

Preserving the Patina

Terracotta is a living material. Proper sealing is not just protection—it's an essential part of the design process.
