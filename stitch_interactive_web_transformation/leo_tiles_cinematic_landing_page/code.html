The Craft

From the Kiln

01

Forged by Hand

In our Kraaifontein studio, every tile begins as raw Cape clay. Our craftsmen use traditional techniques passed down through generations, pressing each piece into wooden molds to ensure the organic character that defines high-end architectural ceramics.

Tempered by Fire

Our specialized kiln process fires tiles at temperatures exceeding 1100°C. This slow, high-heat alchemy creates a palette of deep burnt oranges and charcoal tones—a permanent record of the intense heat and the mineral makeup of the earth.
